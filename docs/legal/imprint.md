# Imprint / Impressum

**Status: draft skeleton.** Written 11 September 2026 as the phase 00 deliverable of the
[commercial plan](../commercial-roadmap.md). Germany requires this page under § 5 DDG, and an
incomplete or missing imprint is itself an offence that competitors may act on. Every
`[BRACKETED]` value below must be filled in from the maintainer's actual registration documents —
nothing here may be guessed — and the whole page must be confirmed in the counsel review that
phase 00 books.

Version: `draft-0`.

## Angaben gemäß § 5 DDG

```text
[LEGAL NAME]
[LEGAL FORM, e.g. Einzelunternehmen / UG (haftungsbeschränkt) / GmbH]
[STREET AND NUMBER]
[POSTCODE] [CITY]
Deutschland
```

## Vertreten durch

`[NAME OF THE MANAGING DIRECTOR OR SOLE TRADER]`

Required only where the legal form has a representative. A sole trader repeats their own name.

## Kontakt

```text
E-Mail:   [SUPPORT EMAIL]
Telefon:  [PHONE]
```

A telephone number, or an equally immediate second contact channel, is required. Confirm in the
counsel review whether an electronic contact form satisfies this in place of a published number,
and whether a published number can be a business VoIP line.

## Registereintrag

```text
Registergericht:  [REGISTER COURT]
Registernummer:   [HRB / HRA NUMBER]
```

Leave this section out entirely if the legal form has no commercial register entry. A sole trader
who is not registered must not invent one.

## Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer

Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer gemäß § 27 a Umsatzsteuergesetz: `[VAT ID]`

If the business is a small business under § 19 UStG and has no VAT identification number, remove
this section rather than leaving a placeholder, and confirm with the accountant whether the
Kleinunternehmer treatment survives selling through a merchant of record.
